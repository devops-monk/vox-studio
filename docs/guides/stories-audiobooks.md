# Stories & audiobooks

Turn a book or story into narrated audio, chapter by chapter, and read along as it plays.

**Audiobook** uses one narrator. **Stories** gives every speaking character their own voice. You can switch any book between the two at any time.

## 1. Import
Open **Audiobook** or **Stories** and use the import card:
- **Choose file**: EPUB, Word (.docx), text or Markdown. You can also drop the file onto the card.
- **Paste text**: lines like *Chapter 2* start a new chapter.

VoxStudio finds the chapters, and in stories, the characters who speak.

## 2. Cast
In the right-hand panel:
- **Narrator**: pick an engine and voice.
- **Characters** (story mode): a voice for each speaking character, or *Narrator's voice*. Each character has a colour, used for their lines in the reader.
- **Pace**: 0.7× to 1.4×.

## 3. Narrate
Click **Narrate book**. Progress shows at the top, and you can **Pause** at any time.
- Each chapter's dot turns **green** when it's narrated, or **orange** if you change its text or voices afterwards.
- **Narrate the rest** continues where it stopped, including after quitting the app.
- You can narrate just one chapter from its page.

## 4. Read along
Select a chapter and press ▶ in the player bar. The sentence being read is highlighted and scrolls into view. **Click any sentence** to jump to it. ⏮ ⏭ move between chapters.

## 5. Export
Once every chapter is narrated:
- **M4B**: a single audiobook file with chapter markers, for Apple Books and most audiobook apps.
- **MP3**: a single file for any player.

The first click creates the file (watch **Activity**); click again to save it.
