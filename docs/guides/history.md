# History

Every take you generate, from Home, Studio, Clone, Design or the API, is kept in **History**.

## Find a take
- **Search** by what was said.
- **All / Starred** shows everything or just your favourites.
- **All engines** filters to one engine.

Takes are grouped by day (**Today**, **Yesterday**, weekdays, then dates). Scroll down and older takes load automatically.

## Work with a take
Each row shows what was said, the voice, the engine, the time and the length. Hover to reveal:
- **☆** star it. Starred takes are never deleted automatically.
- **⤓** save it as a WAV file.
- **🗑** delete it.

Press ▶ to play, or click the waveform to jump to a point.

## Tidy up several at once
1. Click **Select**.
2. Click takes to tick them, or use **Select all**.
3. Click **Delete** in the bar at the bottom.

The header shows how many takes you have and how much disk space they use.

## Recent takes, from anywhere
Click the **panel** button at the top right of any page to open **Recent takes**, your last 12. Click one to play it, or **All →** to open History.

## Keep history tidy automatically
Go to **Settings → History → Keep takes** and choose **Forever**, **90 days**, **30 days** or **7 days**. Older takes are deleted automatically. **Starred takes are always kept**, so star anything you want to keep.
