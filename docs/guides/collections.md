# Collections & tags

Tags work the same way everywhere: on **voices**, **takes** and **projects**. A **collection** is everything that shares a tag. Use one for a show (`#podcast`), a book (`#book-2`), a client or a campaign.

## Tag things
- **Voices**: open a voice in **Voices** and add tags under its details.
- **Takes**: in **History**, hover over a take and click **#**. Type a tag and press Return; suggestions come from tags you already use.
- **Projects**: open a project and click **#** next to its name.

Tags are lower-case, up to 24 characters, with up to 12 per item. `Podcast` and `podcast` are the same tag.

## Browse collections
Open **Collections** in the sidebar. Each tag is listed with how many items it has. Pick one to see its **voices** (with a button to use the voice in Studio), **takes** (playable) and **projects**, all together.

- **Rename** changes the tag on every voice, take and project at once. Renaming onto an existing tag merges the two.
- **Remove** takes the tag off everything. The items themselves are kept.

In **History**, the **All tags** menu filters takes by tag, and clicking a tag chip on a take does the same.
