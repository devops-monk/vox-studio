# Transcribe

Turn recordings and your own voice into text, privately.

## Before you start
Transcription uses **Whisper**. The first time you open **Transcribe**, pick a model to download:
- **Whisper Base** (recommended to start): quick, and great for dictation.
- **Whisper Small**: more accurate for files.
- **Whisper Large v3 Turbo**: the most accurate, for important recordings.

You can install more than one. When transcribing a file, choose which to use from the **Model** menu.

## Transcribe a file
1. Choose **Transcribe a file**.
2. Optionally pick the **language**. *Detect automatically* works well in most cases.
3. Drop an audio or video file onto the box, or click to choose one: MP3, M4A, WAV, MP4, MOV and more, up to 2 GB.
4. Watch the progress. You can leave the page, and it continues in **Activity**.

## Live transcription
1. Choose **Live** and click **Start listening**. Allow microphone access the first time.
2. Speak. Text appears as you talk: grey words are still settling, black words are final.
3. Click **Stop**. The transcript is saved to the list.

## Read, fix and export
Pick a transcript from the list on the left:
- **Click a timestamp** to hear that moment. The sentence being played is highlighted.
- **Double-click a sentence** to correct it. Press Enter to save or Esc to cancel.
- **Click the title** to rename it.
- **TXT · SRT · VTT · JSON** export plain text, subtitles or data.
- **🗑** then **Confirm** deletes it.

Search the list to find any transcript by title or words.
