# Dictation

Type with your voice in **any app**: email, notes, chat, your code editor.

## Use it
1. Click where you want the text to go, in any app.
2. Press **⇧⌘Space** (Shift-Command-Space). A small pill appears at the bottom of your screen, and its orb moves with your voice.
3. Speak.
4. Press **⇧⌘Space** again to finish, or simply stop talking: after about two seconds it finishes on its own.

Your words are typed where your cursor is. Press **Esc** or ✕ to cancel without typing anything.

You can also start dictation from the **VoxStudio menu-bar icon → Start dictation**.

## Let VoxStudio type for you (macOS)
To type into other apps, macOS needs you to allow VoxStudio in **Accessibility**:
1. Open **Settings → Dictation** in VoxStudio.
2. Click **Grant access**. System Settings opens at **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.
3. Turn on **VoxStudio**.

Until then, dictation still works: your words are **copied**, and the pill says *Copied — press ⌘V to paste*.

VoxStudio types by pasting, and it puts back whatever was on your clipboard a moment later, so your clipboard is left as you had it.

## Settings → Dictation
| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shortcut** | Click it, then press a new combination (at least one of ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧ plus a key). |
| **Paste automatically** | Off: your words are only copied. |
| **Finish after a pause** | Off: dictation keeps listening until you press the shortcut again. |
| **Language** | Detect automatically, or fix it to your language for better accuracy. |
| **Try it** | Opens the pill right away. |

## Privacy
Dictation runs entirely on your computer with Whisper. Nothing is sent anywhere, and dictated text isn't saved as a transcript.
