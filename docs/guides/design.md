# Design a voice

Describe a voice in your own words, such as *"a warm, deep British narrator, calm and measured"*, and VoxStudio creates new voices to match. It blends its built-in voices, so the result is something new rather than a stock voice.

## Before you start
Design uses **Kokoro**. The first time, VoxStudio also needs to **analyze its voices**, which takes about a minute and only happens once. Click **Analyze voices** and watch it listen to each one.

## 1. Describe
Type into the large box, or click one of the examples. Underneath, **Understood:** shows how your words were read: for example **Female**, **British**, **Warm**, **Unhurried**.

Words that work well:
- **Who:** woman / man, young, British / American
- **Sound:** deep or light · warm, soft, rich or crisp, clear, bright
- **Mood:** calm, measured, soothing or energetic, lively, upbeat
- **Pace:** slow, unhurried or quick, brisk

## 2. Listen to the candidates
Four candidates appear: **Voice A–D**. Each shows its **match** percentage and bars for depth, warmth and energy. Click **Listen** to hear it say the preview line. You can change the preview line in the box above the candidates.

Click a card to select it.

## 3. Fine-tune by ear
Use the **Fine-tune** sliders:
- **Depth**, **Warmth**, **Energy**: moving one overrides what the description said, and new candidates appear instantly.
- **Pace**: how fast the voice speaks.

**Reset** returns the sliders to your description.

## 4. Save
Name your voice and click **Save voice**. It appears:
- under **Your designed voices** at the bottom of this page, where you can play, use or delete it
- in **Voices**, with a **Designed** badge
- in Studio, under **Your voices** when Kokoro is selected

## Tips
- Be specific about what you care about most. If you only say "calm", only energy is targeted.
- Your pace is saved with the voice, so Studio's Pace slider adjusts it further.
- Design covers English (American and British) for now.
