# Your voice library

**Voices** in the sidebar shows every voice you can use in one place: built-in voices from each engine, and the voices you've cloned.

## Browse
- **Search** by name, language or tag.
- **All / Favorites / Yours / Built-in** narrows the list.
- **All engines** limits the list to one engine.
- **#tags** appear once you've tagged something. Click one to filter; click it again to clear.

Favorites come first, then your own voices, then natural-sounding neural voices, with system voices last.

## Preview
Hover over a voice's avatar and press ▶.
- Built-in voices speak a short introduction. The first preview takes a moment; replays are instant.
- Your cloned voices play the **original recording** they were made from.

## Favorite
Click ☆ on a card, or **Add to favorites** in its details. Favorites appear at the top of the library, and in a **Favorites** section at the top of Studio's voice list.

## Details
Click a card to open its details panel:
- **Rename** (your voices): click the name and type.
- **Tags**: type a tag and press Enter. Backspace in an empty box removes the last tag. Existing tags are suggested as you type.
- **Consent record** (your voices): the consent statement, who gave it, and when.
- **Use in Studio**: opens Studio with this voice selected.
- **Export** (your voices): saves a `.voxvoice` file you can back up or share.
- **Delete** (your voices): click, then **Confirm delete**. Takes you already made are kept.

Press **Esc** or ✕ to close the panel.

## Share and import voices
**Export** creates a `.voxvoice` file containing the recording, name, tags and consent record.

To import one:
1. Click **Import** at the top of Voices and choose the `.voxvoice` file.
2. Confirm you have permission to use the voice, and optionally who gave it.
3. Click **Import**.

Your confirmation is saved together with the voice's original consent record, so its history is never lost.

> Cloned voices need **Chatterbox** to speak. Without it, they still appear in the library (dimmed) and you can still preview their original recordings.
