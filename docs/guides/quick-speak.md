# Quick Speak & Speak Selection

## Quick Speak: type it, hear it
Press **⌃⌥S** anywhere on your Mac. A small glass panel opens near the top of the screen, like Spotlight.

- Type, then press **Return** to hear it. **Shift-Return** adds a new line.
- **↑ / ↓** bring back phrases you spoke earlier in this session.
- **Esc** stops playback. Press it again to close the panel. Clicking elsewhere also closes it, and audio keeps playing.
- Pick a voice from the menu at the bottom-left. Your choice is remembered, and it's the same “quick voice” as in **Settings → General**.
- **Replay** plays the last result again. **Open in Studio** moves the text into Studio for fine-tuning.

Everything you speak is saved in **History**. Quick Speak is also in the menu-bar icon's menu and under **View → Quick Speak**.

## Speak Selection: read anything aloud
Select text in any app, such as a web page, an email or a PDF, and press **⌃⌥R**. VoxStudio copies the selection, puts your clipboard back the way it was, and reads the text in the Quick Speak panel. Press **Esc** to stop.

Speak Selection needs the same **Accessibility** permission as dictation: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → VoxStudio**. Without it, the panel tells you what to enable.

## Change the shortcuts
**Settings → Shortcuts** lets you record new shortcuts for Quick Speak, Speak Selection and Dictation. Each needs at least one modifier key (⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧).
