# Batch & watch folders

Process lots of material at once, or have VoxStudio handle new files as they arrive.

## Speak many
1. Open **Batch → Speak many**.
2. Paste your texts: **one per line**, or separate longer passages with a **blank line**. You can also **Add .txt files**.
3. Choose an engine and a voice.
4. Optionally click **Save files to a folder…** to get a WAV file per item there.
5. Click **Speak**.

Every item appears in **Recent batches** with overall progress, and individually in **Activity**. Everything you make is also kept in **History**.

## Transcribe many
1. Open **Batch → Transcribe many** and click **Choose files…** to pick recordings or videos.
2. Choose what to write: **TXT**, **SRT**, **VTT** and/or **JSON**.
3. Optionally choose a folder for the files, then click **Transcribe**.

Transcripts also appear in **Transcribe**.

## Watch folders
Let a folder do the work: drop a file in, and the result appears next to it.
1. Open **Batch → Watch folders**.
2. Choose **Speak text files** (and a voice) or **Transcribe recordings**.
3. Click **Watch a folder…** and pick a folder.

From then on, new files are handled automatically, and the results appear in a **VoxStudio output** folder inside it. Each card shows the latest files and whether they finished (✓) or had a problem (⚠).
- Use the switch to **pause** a folder, and 🗑 to stop watching it.
- Files are only picked up once they've finished copying, and each file is handled just once.

> **Tip:** watch a synced folder (iCloud Drive, Dropbox) to send scripts from your phone and have audio ready when you're back at your Mac.
